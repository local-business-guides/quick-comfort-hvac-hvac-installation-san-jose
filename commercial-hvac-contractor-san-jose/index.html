Scope, process, and useful terms

A provider typically needs details about the system and the problem before discussing the appropriate service. For an installation, clarify what equipment and related work are included. For maintenance or repair, ask what will be checked, what the assessment found, and whether further work may be needed. Timing and cost can’t be set from a general description alone because system condition and job scope vary.

Terms you may hear during a commercial HVAC discussion:
- Rooftop unit: A packaged heating and cooling system installed on a roof. Ask whether the provider works on the specific unit at your site.
- Load: The heating or cooling demand a system needs to meet. Building use and conditions can affect that demand.
- Preventive maintenance: Planned service intended to check and care for equipment, rather than address only a reported breakdown.

Use these terms as prompts for clarification. Ask the provider to explain any recommendation in plain language and identify what is included.
