What HVAC service can include

An HVAC contractor may inspect, maintain, repair, or install heating and air conditioning equipment. The work depends on your system and the concern you report. For a repair, the contractor first needs to understand the symptoms and assess the equipment. Maintenance is intended to check system condition, while installation involves fitting new equipment and confirming the work required for your setup.

A few terms you may hear:
- HVAC: Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning.
- Heat pump: Equipment that can provide heating and cooling by moving heat.
- Maintenance: Scheduled service to check and care for equipment; it is different from repairing a known fault.

Ask what the visit includes, whether any parts or additional work may be needed, and how the contractor will explain findings. These questions help you compare proposals on the same basis.
