What HVAC service covers, timing, and cost

HVAC service can include installing a system, maintaining existing equipment, or repairing a fault. The right next step depends on what the system is doing and what an inspection finds. A provider should explain the recommendation before work begins.

Key facts to discuss:
- Scope: Ask whether the visit is for a repair, routine maintenance, or installation, and what each part of the job includes.
- Timing: The time needed depends on the type of work, the condition of the equipment, and whether parts or additional work are needed. Ask for a job-specific schedule.
- Cost factors: Equipment choice, the work required, system condition, and parts can affect the total. Request a written explanation of the proposed work and its cost before approving it.
- Credentials: Ask which certification or other qualification applies to the work, and how you can verify it.

A few terms you may hear: HVAC means heating, ventilation, and air conditioning. Installation is putting in a system or equipment. Maintenance is planned care intended to check and service equipment. Repair addresses a problem with a system that is already in place.
